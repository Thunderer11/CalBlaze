\# 🔥 CalBlaze — Smart Calorie \& Nutrition Tracker



\*\*Track what you eat. Know what you fuel.\*\*



CalBlaze is a calorie and nutrition tracking application designed to make food tracking simple, flexible, and accurate, with a special focus on \*\*Indian cuisine and homemade meals\*\*.



Unlike traditional calorie trackers that rely heavily on generic serving sizes, CalBlaze aims to let users calculate nutrition based on the exact ingredients and quantities used in their meals, including cooking oil, vegetables, spices, and other ingredients.



\## ✨ Key Features



\### 🇮🇳 Indian Food Database

\- Extensive coverage of Indian foods, including roti, dal, sabzi, paneer, paratha, rice, and regional dishes.

\- Search foods and view their estimated calories and macronutrients.

\- Support for different serving sizes and quantities.



\### 🥘 Custom Recipe Calculator

\- Create meals using individual ingredients.

\- Specify exact quantities in grams, millilitres, or suitable household measures.

\- Include cooking oil, ghee, butter, vegetables, spices, and other ingredients.

\- Calculate total calories, protein, carbohydrates, and fats.

\- Calculate nutrition per serving or per 100 grams based on the final cooked weight.

\- Save frequently prepared meals for future tracking.



\### 📊 Nutrition Dashboard

\- Track daily calorie intake and remaining calories.

\- Monitor protein, carbohydrates, and fats.

\- Log breakfast, lunch, dinner, and snacks.

\- View progress toward personalized nutrition goals.



\### 📈 Progress Tracking

\- Review daily and weekly nutrition summaries.

\- Track body weight and nutrition trends.

\- Maintain historical food logs and saved recipes.

\## 🧮 Nutrition Calculation



CalBlaze will use ingredient-level nutritional data to estimate the nutritional value of homemade meals.



\*\*Basic formula:\*\*



`Nutrient contribution = (Nutrient per 100 g × Ingredient quantity) / 100`



The application will combine the nutritional contributions of individual ingredients to calculate the total recipe values. Final cooked weight and serving size will be considered when calculating nutrition per serving or per 100 grams.
